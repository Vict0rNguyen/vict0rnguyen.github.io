Personal apps by Vict0rNguyen

Small apps that keep your data on your own devices and in your own Google Drive. Each app has its own page and privacy policy.

  • Vaccine Book (Sổ Tiêm Chủng)
    Keeps track of your children's vaccinations and appointments (Android). Privacy policy
  • CalendarProMax
    A Vietnamese lunar calendar for Android and Windows. Privacy policy
  • SpendBook (Sổ chi tiêu)
    Records your spending from the messages of your banks and e-wallets (Android). Privacy policy
  • People Book (Sổ Người Thân)
    Birthdays, death anniversaries, keeping in touch and a gift ledger (Android). Privacy policy
  • Secretary (Thư ký)
    One morning briefing: lunar calendar, meetings, birthdays, weather, gold, deposit rates, news, spending and Azure DevOps and Jira work (Android and Windows). Privacy policy
